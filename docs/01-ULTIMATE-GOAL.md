# TOEFL House — Ultimate Goal / North Star

**STATUS: CURRENT CANONICAL — NORMATIVE**

## Ultimate Goal

TOEFL House is intended to become a single, trustworthy, multi-branch education-management platform in which the complete organizational, academic, financial, people, operational, communication and reporting lifecycles are governed by explicit authorities, enforceable invariants, attributable decisions and recoverable history — while presenting employees and students with a coherent, efficient experience and allowing the platform to evolve without creating competing business truths.

## Product outcome

The platform should let an education organization operate the full journey from prospective visitor/admission through student lifecycle, academic delivery, assessment, placement/progression/graduation, billing and payment, people/payroll settlement, operational work and management reporting.

## Permanent properties

- one owner for every material business fact;
- one canonical mutation path for each stateful authority;
- finance owns monetary truth;
- academic owns academic lifecycle truth;
- organization owns organizational topology;
- access owns effective identity, authorization and scope;
- derived/reporting data never becomes a competing authority;
- important invariants are enforced at the strongest practical boundary, including PostgreSQL;
- sensitive actions are attributable, scoped and reviewable;
- retries and concurrent requests do not create duplicate business facts;
- historical facts are corrected through governed corrections, not destructive rewrites;
- release claims are evidence-backed.

## Maturity target

The end state includes complete approved product breadth, full target frontend migration, mature projections/integration operations, production-grade observability and disaster recovery, comprehensive testing evidence, and an independently verifiable release process.

The target is not “more features.” It is a coherent system in which business truth, authority, user experience and operational reliability remain aligned as the organization grows.

## Users and outcomes

The platform serves owners and governance leaders, campus/branch managers, admissions/reception staff, academic staff and teachers, finance staff, HR/payroll staff, CRM and operations staff, auditors, and students where approved student-facing surfaces exist. Each role should complete its work through governed capabilities rather than through direct manipulation of business truth.

## Organizational outcome

An organization can operate multiple campuses and branches with effective-dated topology, explicit scope propagation, attributable authority and historical provenance. Branch context is a business fact, not merely a UI filter.

## Educational outcome

The platform governs the student journey from admission through placement, enrollment, delivery, assessment, progression, graduation and transcript issuance, with evidence and financial gates attached to their canonical authorities.

## Financial outcome

Every recorded monetary fact is attributable to Finance, traceable to its source transaction or recognized liability, protected against duplicate settlement and concurrency errors, and correctable without erasing the original fact.

## People and operations outcome

Employees work through coherent workspaces, queues, tasks, approvals and notifications that coordinate activity without becoming shadow business authorities. CRM and operational workflows feed canonical admissions and domain processes.

## Data and governance outcome

Every material fact has an owner, write authority, provenance and correction path. Derived data is rebuildable where required. Audit records prove what happened or was attempted without pretending to be the domain event stream.

## Reliability outcome

Retries, duplicate submissions, concurrent requests, stale state, partial downstream failure and recovery do not silently create duplicate or contradictory business facts. Strong invariants live at the database boundary whenever practical.

## Integration outcome

Transactional domain facts may be projected and delivered to downstream consumers through outbox/consumer patterns without allowing integrations, reports or workflow surfaces to mint business truth.

## Experience outcome

Operators experience one coherent system rather than a collection of modules. The target interactive boundary is React/TypeScript over canonical APIs, with remaining Blade limited to explicit transitional/non-interactive or compatibility responsibilities until retirement criteria are met.

## Evolution outcome

The system can evolve for years without losing provenance or accumulating competing authorities. New capabilities must attach to existing ownership and governance rather than creating parallel truth models.

## Quality outcome

The project does not call itself release-ready from static inspection alone. Production readiness requires evidence from the official runtime, database, tests, concurrency checks, frontend build/integration and deployment/recovery gates defined by the release documentation.
