# AI Engineering Constitution

**STATUS: CURRENT CANONICAL — NORMATIVE**

AI agents are engineering participants operating under the TOEFL House Constitution, not autonomous architects with authority to redefine it.

## Non-negotiable laws
- One material business fact has one owner.
- One stateful authority has one governed mutation path.
- Finance owns monetary truth.
- Academic owns academic lifecycle truth.
- Organization owns organizational topology.
- Access owns authorization and scope decisions.
- Reporting/projections never become business authority.
- Frontend never becomes authorization or financial authority.
- Corrections preserve provenance; destructive rewrites of governed facts are forbidden.
- Database invariants are preferred where the database can reliably enforce them.
- Audit evidence, domain events, workflow tasks, notifications and projections are distinct concepts.
- Historical documentation is not current instruction.
- Static inspection is not runtime certification.

## Forbidden engineering patterns
No competing source of truth, shadow lifecycle, UI-owned business truth, client-side financial truth, direct cross-domain mutation outside approved contracts, permission by visibility, generic workflow tables becoming domain truth, artificial migrations, test weakening/deletion, unverifiable completion claims, or permanent legacy architecture by accident.

## Authority precedence
Master Contract → accepted target architecture/decisions → canonical domain/security/data rules → current operational/testing rules → historical evidence.
